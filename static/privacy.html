Privacy Policy for Photovance AI

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Welcome to Photovance AI ("we", "our", or "app"). Your privacy and the security of your visual identity are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how our application processes, handles, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related AI services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide AI-powered photo transformation, outfit styling, and portrait rendering, the application accesses:

  • User-Selected Images: Only the photos you explicitly select from your device gallery or capture using your camera. We never scan, browse, or access your photo library without your direct action.
  • Camera Access: Used solely to allow you to take a portrait/selfie directly within the app for outfit transformation.
  • Text Prompts: Fashion and scene descriptions you enter to generate your desired styles.

2. How Your Images are Processed

  • Temporary AI Inference: Photos you provide are transmitted securely to our AI neural engine solely to render your requested outfit, lighting, and scene.
  • Biometric Facial Identity: Facial landmark analysis is performed strictly in real-time to preserve your authentic facial features, smile, and expression on the new outfit.
  • No Commercial Sale: We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal photos or biometric facial embeddings to any third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.
  • Zero Data Retention: Temporary input images are deleted after workflow completion.

3. Third-Party Services & Google Play Compliance

Photovance AI complies fully with Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the User Data Policy and Data Safety requirements. Communication between the mobile application and processing endpoints is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.
